# test_magic_attack.txt (Corrected)
# Test Magic Damage, AS calculation, and Fatigue with Fire Tome equipped.

# Setup: Leif (pcc=1, mag=5, Fire Tome equipped) vs Bandit (pcc=0, mag=0)
# Fire Tome: Magic, Mt 6, Hit 85, Crit 0, Wt 4, Rng 1-2

# --- Initial State Info ---
info 1 4 # Leif - Check equipped weapon is Fire Tome
info 5 4 # Bandit

# --- Test Magic Attack ---
# Move Leif into range first, then equip Fire Tome
select 1 4
move 4 4 # Move Leif to (4,4), adjacent to Bandit at (5,4)
equip 1 # Equip item at index 1 (Fire Tome)

# Now attack with Fire Tome
# Leif AS (Magic) = Spd 7 - Wt 4 = 3
# Bandit AS = 4
# Leif does NOT double (3 < 4+4)
# Leif Hit = Tome Hit 85 + 2*Skl 6 + Luk 6 = 85 + 12 + 6 = 103 -> 99%
# Bandit Avoid = 2*AS 4 + Luk 0 = 8%
# Leif Battle Hit = 99 - 8 = 91%
# Leif Base Crit = Tome Crit 0 + Skl 6 = 6% -> Battle Crit (1st hit) = 6% (capped at 6%)
# Leif Damage = (Mag 5 + Mt 6) - Bandit Mag 0 = 11
attack 5 4
# Expect: Leif attacks Bandit with Fire Tome. Fatigue +1. Deals 11 damage. Low crit chance (6%).
# Expect: Bandit cannot counter (Range 1 vs Range 1-2).

# --- Check Final State ---
info 4 4 # Check Leif HP/Fatigue at new position
info 5 4 # Check Bandit HP/Fatigue

endturn
quit
